Q&A

How long are the sessions, and how many students per class?

Pyhon classes are 1 hour and our Website Making classes are two hours. All sessions are one-time only, meaning that they do not continue off of each other. courses have a limited quantity of 10 students due to computer quanity.


When should my child be dropped off?

We reserve the spaces for a specific time with ten minutes ahead of the class. This means that you can drop off your children 10 minutes ahead of time. NOTE: If you come ahead of time, your teacher will not be there.
